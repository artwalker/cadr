## B3 string enums (`"none"` / `"edge"`)

Two families, both kept as strings:

1. **Canvas `Span.cls`** (`"none"`, `"edge"`, `"border"`, `"text"`,
   `"edgeLabel"`, `"title"`).  These are grok-mermaid class names.
   They are not printed.  They *are* the `mermaid.ts:39-53` styling
   contract (`edge` → accent, `none` → unstyled).
2. **Parser arrowhead slots** (`parse.lisp` `+gm-class-ops+`,
   `head-to` / `head-from`).  `"none"` means "do not emit a head
   glyph".  That changes the art bytes.  It is an output contract.

Neither is leftover TypeScript style that can be deleted without a
paired rewrite of writers and goldens.
